# 📱 Social Media Do’s and Don’ts Checklist
### Responsible Online Behavior for College Students

---

## ✅ DO’s
- **Think before you post** – once something is online, it can stay there forever.
- **Use respectful language** while commenting, messaging, or posting.
- **Fact-check information** before sharing news, updates, or screenshots.
- **Keep your profile professional** because recruiters and professors may view it.
- **Protect your privacy** by limiting what personal details you share.

---

## ❌ DON’Ts
- **Don’t cyberbully or troll others** through comments or messages.
- **Don’t share fake news, rumors, or misleading content.**
- **Don’t post private chats or photos without permission.**
- **Don’t overshare personal information** like address, phone number, or passwords.
- **Don’t post anything in anger** that you may regret later.

---

## 💡 Golden Rule
**Post only what you would be comfortable showing to your teacher, parents, and future employer.**
